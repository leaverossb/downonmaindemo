What makes Down on Main special is the community. The people who come through the doors bring so much love, creativity and willingness to build something together. We may not be perfect or polished, but there’s always someone with an idea, an artist with something to share, a musician ready to play, or a friend pulling up another friend. We want you to feel at home when you walk through the door, because this is our home. Built in the small town we love by our friends and family.

So come on in! Let us cook you a meal or make you a drink. Whether you are alone or with friends and family, everyone has a seat at our table. Listen to music, watch the game, play billiards, sing karaoke, bring an instrument for open mic, or just hang out and enjoy the vibes. We look forward to seeing you soon!
